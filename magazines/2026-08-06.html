AVIATION DAILY

Thursday, August 6, 2026

Rolls-Royce's second guidance raise of the year landed in the same week as a fresh Trent 1000 airworthiness directive, a reminder that even engine programs still climbing the durability curve are minting record aftermarket revenue. 01

Rolls-Royce raises full-year guidance again as profit surges 46%

Bloomberg
Bloomberg · July 30, 2026 · read →

Rolls-Royce lifted its full-year outlook for the second time in 2026, now guiding to adjusted operating profit of £4.7–4.9 billion and free cash flow of up to £4 billion after underlying profit jumped 46% to £2.5 billion in the first half. The surge was driven by aftermarket demand across civil and military engines plus a fast-growing Power Systems business selling into AI data centers.

Pratt & Whitney spreads $100M+ across four GTF MRO sites, plus $100M more in Poland

Aviation Week
Aviation Week Network · 2026 · read →

Pratt & Whitney detailed a wave of GTF shop investments: a $20 million, 40%-capacity expansion at its West Palm Beach Engine Center, a $78 million used-serviceable-material facility in Irving, Texas that opened last fall, a $4.7 million case-repair expansion in Springdale, Arkansas, and a new $100 million forging plant in Rzeszów, Poland due in 2028. PW1100G shop output is already up 23% year-over-year, though active groundings remain above 400 aircraft.

FAA tightens Trent 1000 air-transfer-tube inspections, supersedes 2023 directive

Federal Register
Federal Aviation Administration · effective July 9, 2026 · read →

A new AD covering the Trent 1000 family adds initial and repetitive on-wing visual inspections of the IP8 and HP3 air transfer tubes for cracking, damage or leakage, replacing a 2023 directive. In exchange, operators get a longer interval on the equivalent in-shop inspection — a sign the underlying fix is maturing even as the paperwork gets more granular.

AviTrader
AviTrader · July 29, 2026 · read →

Bird Aviation will run two dedicated maintenance lines for easyJet's A320-family fleet out of its Larnaca facility under a seven-year agreement, with an option to extend three more years. The deal builds on an existing relationship and cements Larnaca's position as a heavy-maintenance hub for the eastern Mediterranean.

Engine borescope inspection market set to nearly double by 2033 as AI moves into the shop

Market Research
Market intelligence report · 2026 · read →

Analysts value the global engine borescope inspection services market at $0.85 billion in 2024 and project it will reach $1.58 billion by 2033, a 7.1% annual growth rate. The forecast tracks a broader shift toward videoscopes that produce engineering-grade 3D measurements and serial-number-linked defect records instead of just pictures.

3D Printing Industry · July 7, 2026 · read →

The partnership pairs EOS's printer ecosystem with Constellium's alloy expertise, starting with a new powder — EOS Aluminium Constellium CP1 — pitched as a higher-strength, simpler-to-heat-treat alternative to AlSi10Mg due for release this month. Air Force presses GE and Pratt & Whitney to guarantee fighter-engine output

Aviation Week
Aviation Week Network · August 3, 2026 · read →

A new market-research solicitation asks both F110 maker GE Aerospace and F100 maker Pratt & Whitney to detail how they'd scale production to a projected peak of 180 engines a year by 2034 for F-15EX and F-16 programs. The Air Force cited "production delays, quality control issues and critical obsolescence" in the current fighter-engine industrial base as the reason for a strategic recompete.
