Revised AD tightens inspection window for cracking PW210 turbine exhaust frames

Federal Register
Federal Register · effective Aug 31, 2026 · read →

The FAA has revised its directive for Pratt & Whitney Canada PW210A, PW210A1, and PW210S engines after further reports of circumferential cracking on the turbine exhaust frame's external surface, driven by thermal stress from repeated engine starts. The new AD supersedes AD 2026-13-09 to catch frames cracking earlier than the original directive anticipated, and takes effect August 31, giving operators of the affected fleet a compressed compliance window.

Pratt targets full GTF recovery by decade's end as Advantage ramps

FlightGlobal
FlightGlobal · Aug 2026 · read →

Pratt & Whitney says the PW1100G-JM fleet's aircraft-on-ground problem, which has averaged roughly 350 grounded jets a day through 2026, won't be fully resolved until closer to 2030 even as inspection throughput keeps improving. The GTF Advantage — 4% more takeoff thrust, better fuel burn, and more durable hot-section hardware than the baseline engine — is now the centerpiece of that recovery as production ramps and Pratt works to lock in delivery volumes with Airbus.

MRO Business Today
MRO Business Today · Aug 2026 · read →

A new generation of videoscopes is pushing engine borescope work beyond subjective visual calls toward engineering-grade 3D measurement and quantitative defect sizing tied directly to an engine's serial number and cycle count. Paired with AI-assisted defect recognition and remote visual inspection, the shift promises more consistent go/no-go decisions right as GTF and LEAP shop-visit volume stretches qualified borescope inspectors thin.

GE Aerospace News
GE Aerospace News · Aug 2026 · read →

Rolls-Royce and Reliance plan a sovereign fighter engine for India's AMCA

Rolls-Royce
Rolls-Royce · Aug 14, 2026 · read →

Rolls-Royce and Reliance Industries have announced strategic intent to jointly design, develop and manufacture a fully Indian-owned combat engine for the Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft — a clean-sheet 120kN design meant to enable supercruise. The pair are also exploring a dedicated Aerospace Gas Turbine Complex in India spanning design through overhaul, a long-horizon bet that signals Rolls-Royce building sovereign engine capability beyond its traditional big-fan business.

Embraer's backlog hits a record $34.5B on its seventh straight quarterly high

Embraer
Embraer · Aug 2026 · read →

Embraer closed the second quarter with a record $34.5 billion backlog, up 7% from Q1 and 16% year-on-year, with commercial, defense and executive aviation all posting gains. The E2 program crossed 500 firm orders on the back of an Azorra order for 15 E195-E2s, while the defense backlog jumped 42% on a UAE C-390 Millennium deal, alongside the company's strongest second-quarter delivery total in 16 years.

Fuel spike guts European airline profits in the second quarter

FlightGlobal
FlightGlobal · Aug 14, 2026 · read →

Collective operating profits roughly halved among Europe's leading carriers in the April-June quarter as jet fuel, pushed above $160 a barrel by the Iran conflict, bit hard into margins. IATA now projects the region's full-year net profit falling to $9.6 billion from $13 billion in 2025, with profit per passenger dropping from $10.30 to $7.50 — a reminder that OEMs' record backlogs still sit downstream of airline economics that can turn fast.
