FAA orders inspections of Rolls-Royce Trent XWB fuel manifold hoses after in-flight engine fire

Federal Register
Federal Aviation Administration · effective July 17, 2026 · read →

The directive covers Trent XWB-75, -79, -79B, -84 and -97 engines after a main fuel hose assembly degraded in service, triggering a controlled in-flight fire, heat damage to the nacelle thrust-reverser C-ducts, and a commanded engine shutdown. It supersedes a January 2025 AD with tightened inspection and replacement requirements — the XWB-97 powers the A350-1000, the -84 the A350-900.

Federal Register
Federal Aviation Administration · effective August 21, 2026 · read →

The AD follows a 787-8 fuel imbalance caused by a cracked fuel tube hose linking the main fuel-oil heat exchanger, which GE traced to a defective batch of hose preforms from specific manufacturing lots.
