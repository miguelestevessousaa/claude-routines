FAA orders GEnx-1B/-2B fuel-hose replacement after in-flight leak

federalregister.gov
Federal Register / FAA · Effective Aug 21, 2026 · read →

The new AD was prompted by a Boeing 787-8 that suffered a fuel imbalance after a defective fuel tube hose leaked at the main fuel-oil heat exchanger. It requires operators of a long list of GEnx-1B and GEnx-2B variants to remove all affected hoses from service and replace them with eligible parts before the effective date.

P&W rejects open fan, bets NextGen2 GTF stays a ducted turbofan

leehamnews.com
Leeham News and Analysis · Jul 16, 2026 · read →

Rick Deurloo told reporters at Farnborough that Pratt & Whitney looked hard at unducted, open-rotor architecture for its next narrowbody engine and walked away, calling the geared-turbofan path "a low-risk next-gen solution." He noted Rolls-Royce's UltraFan and GE's turbofan alternative to its own open-fan program as validation that ducted designs aren't going away.
